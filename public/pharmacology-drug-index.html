DRUG

🎨 Edit Color

Bethanechol. Also listed as Bethanecol in chart. Direct muscarinic agonist.

2

MODE OF ACTION

🎨 Edit Color

Stimulates muscarinic receptors, increasing bladder detrusor contraction and gastrointestinal motility.

3

DOSAGE AND SIZE AVAILABLE

🎨 Edit Color
FORMS / STRENGTHS

Oral tablets 5, 10, 25, 50 mg.

DOSE / SCOPE

Adult nonobstructive urinary retention 10–50 mg orally 3–4 times daily, individualized to response.

4

INDICATION

🎨 Edit Color

Postoperative/postpartum nonobstructive urinary retention, neurogenic bladder atony.

5

CONTRAINDICATION

🎨 Edit Color

Hypersensitivity, hyperthyroidism, peptic ulcer, latent or active asthma, pronounced bradycardia or hypotension, vasomotor instability, coronary artery disease, epilepsy or parkinsonism. Do not use with mechanical GI/urinary obstruction or when contraction may harm recent surgery or a weakened wall, consult the product label for the complete exclusions.

6

ADVERSE REACTION

🎨 Edit Color

Bronchospasm, marked bradycardia or hypotension, cholinergic toxicity.
